**3. [HIGH, silent-failure] `os.Stat` non-ENOENT errors take the unlocked branch, defeating TOCTOU protection.**
`internal/mission/store.go` around line 1043-1045. Current check `if _, statErr := os.Stat(missionDir); statErr != nil` catches EACCES, EIO, ELOOP, ENAMETOOLONG etc. and unlocks anyway — the mission dir may actually be present. Only `fs.ErrNotExist` (or equivalently `os.IsNotExist(statErr)`, which is the idiom already used at store.go:684, 1074, 1642, 1653) is the "legitimately absent" signal. Both silent-failure-hunter and code-reviewer flagged this. Fix: use `errors.Is(statErr, fs.ErrNotExist)` or `os.IsNotExist(statErr)` — one-word fix. On any other stat error, log AND fall through to the locked branch (or the new fallback from finding #2), never to the unlocked bare path.
